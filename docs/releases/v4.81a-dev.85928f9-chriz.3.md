# v4.81a-dev.85928f9-chriz.3

This release lets the Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster sorcerers and the
Favored Soul keep their extra kit weapons when they level up, with the
EEex-based weapon usability. It changes new installations only. CEBG manages
its artifact pin separately; this owner release does not update the collection
recipe or any installed game.

## Changes

- **Kit weapons survive `C0PR#CL`.** With the EEex-based weapon usability,
  `C0PR#CL.SPL` first removes the effects of every weapon grant spell and then
  grants the weapons of the character's proficiency pips and class again. The
  game applies it at every level-up, when a character joins the party and when
  a creature is created. Kitpack's kit chains in the helper spell `C0PR#XX.SPL`
  apply the same grant spells, so these kits lost their extra weapons each time,
  until Kitpack's helper creature appeared again (after loading a save or on
  entering an area):
  - Eldritch Knight sorcerer: every weapon type.
  - Arcane Trickster sorcerer: long and short swords, katanas, scimitars,
    clubs, crossbows and short bows. A newly created Arcane Trickster sorcerer
    had none of them until the helper appeared, because the kit's level 1
    abilities do not include its chain.
  - Favored Soul: maces and crossbows. Its removal of axes, scimitars and short
    bows was not affected.

  Kitpack now builds `C0PR#KG.SPL` from `C0PR#XX.SPL` and makes "apply
  `C0PR#KG`" the last effect of every `C0PR#CL` ability, so those chains run
  again right after `C0PR#CL`, under their own class and kit conditions.
  `C0PR#KG` holds only the chains that apply or remove a weapon grant that
  `C0PR#CL` clears; the Monk, Mystic Fire and other chains without weapon grants
  stay out. No kit gains a weapon it did not have before. The new
  `ArtisansKitpack/eeex/kit_weapon_grants.tpa` does this for the first-run
  generator (`ArtisansKitpack/eeex/profs_eeex.tpa`), the "Update EEex-based
  Weapon Usability Changes" tweak (`ArtisansKitpack/lib/profs_eeex_patch.tpa`)
  and the Eldritch Knight sorcerer, Arcane Trickster sorcerer thief equipment
  and Favored Soul components, right after each of them registers its chain.
  Running it again without changes changes nothing.
- **Chains of other mods.** A chain that another mod registers in `C0PR#XX`
  after Kitpack, such as the Mage/Bard of Bardic Wonders (component `2009`),
  joins `C0PR#KG` when Kitpack's "Update EEex-based Weapon Usability Changes"
  tweak (`1010000`) is installed after that mod. Until then it behaves as
  before. A chain that applies `C0PR#CL` or `C0PR#KG` again, or that cannot be
  read, stays out with an installer warning, and the installer warns instead of
  patching a `C0PR#CL.SPL` whose tables it cannot extend safely.
- **Existing installations** are not changed by this release, and there is no
  tail for it. There the helper creature still gives the weapons back as before.

The previously released balance and sorcerer weapon changes are unchanged.

## Validation and installation boundary

October 26 results: all 111 offline tests passed, including 14 new WeiDU tests
in synthetic games. They run the real generator, the tweak and the real chain
blocks of the Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster sorcerers, the Favored Soul,
the Monk and the Mystic Fire next to synthetic chains of other mods, in the
order CEBG installs them. A small model of the weapon grant opcodes shows the
reproduced loss after a level-up before the fix and, after it, the same weapons
as after the helper's pass. They also cover repeated runs, a chain that a later
mod registers before the tweak, chains through EFF files, chains that lead back
to `C0PR#CL`, unreadable and missing spells, duplicate entries, a `C0PR#CL` with
several abilities and casting effects, rejected layouts and uninstalling. All
three installer versions match the release identity.

The in-game lab check is recorded after it has run.

No live game or save was changed for this release.
