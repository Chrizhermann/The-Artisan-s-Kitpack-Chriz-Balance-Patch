# v4.81a-dev.85928f9-chriz.2

This release lets sorcerers use daggers, quarterstaves, darts and slings again
with the EEex-based weapon usability. CEBG manages its artifact pin separately;
this owner release does not update the collection recipe or any installed game.

## Changes

- **Sorcerers can equip daggers, quarterstaves, darts and slings without
  proficiency pips again**, like mages. This affects every sorcerer kit, the
  Dragon Disciple included, whenever the EEex weapon usability is installed.
  Since upstream commit
  [7464043](https://github.com/TheArtisanBG/The-Artisan-s-Kitpack/commit/7464043fbf93fbd448401436181713de3737b72e)
  (February 21), the class weapon grants in `C0PR#CL.SPL` are generated from
  the columns of `WEAPPROF.2DA`. That table has no sorcerer column, because the
  engine gives sorcerers the mage column, so sorcerers received no class
  weapons at all. Sorcerers now use the mage column in both the first-run
  generator (`ArtisansKitpack/eeex/profs_eeex.tpa`) and the "Update EEex-based
  Weapon Usability Changes" tweak (`ArtisansKitpack/lib/profs_eeex_patch.tpa`).
  Mages and all other classes keep their grants unchanged.
- **Existing installations** can append the new
  [AKCB_SORCERER_WEAPONS](../../live-patch/AKCB_SORCERER_WEAPONS/README.md)
  tail instead of reinstalling. It adds the same sorcerer grants to the
  installed `C0PR#CL.SPL` and keeps everything else in that file. Saves need no
  edits: characters receive the grants the next time `C0PR#CL` is applied to
  them, at their next level-up at the latest.

The previously released balance changes are unchanged.

## Validation and installation boundary

October 26 results: all 97 offline tests passed, including 14 new WeiDU tests in
synthetic games. They run the real generator, the tweak and the tail: grants per
class from `WEAPPROF`, a changed mage column, the gated item to stat to grant
chain, the reproduced regression before the fix, a tail result identical to a
fresh fixed install, old-style and partial grants, several abilities and
rejection of malformed input. All three installer versions match the release
identity.

In-game lab check, also on October 26: a disposable copy of an EET game
(BG2:EE 2.7.3.0, EEex 1.3.1) with Kitpack's EEex-based weapon usability, updated
with this release's `AKCB_SORCERER_WEAPONS` tail. Five new level-1 characters
without pips in these weapons (a sorcerer, a Dragon Disciple, a mage, an
Eldritch Knight sorcerer and an Arcane Trickster sorcerer) moved a dagger, a
quarterstaff, darts, a short sword and a long sword from the backpack into a
weapon slot in the inventory screen. This ran after they joined the party,
after Kitpack's helper creature had appeared again, after a level-up to level 2
and after loading that save in a new game process. Each time all five equipped
the dagger, the quarterstaff and the darts, and the game refused both swords to
the sorcerer, the Dragon Disciple and the mage ("Cannot use item."). The mage
results are the same as before the fix, and the mage grants in `C0PR#CL.SPL`
stayed byte-identical. Slings were not checked separately in the game, because
the test characters had their one pip in slings; the offline tests cover the
sorcerer sling grant. Characters created before the tail was installed were not
part of the lab check. The lab shows that a level-up applies `C0PR#CL` again,
and that gives them the grants.

Known issue, unchanged by this release: Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster
sorcerers lose their extra kit weapons, such as swords, whenever `C0PR#CL` runs,
for example at every level-up, because the spell first clears all weapon
grants. Kitpack's helper creature gives them back the next time it appears: in
the lab after loading a save, and by Kitpack's scripts also on entering an area.
A newly created Arcane Trickster sorcerer also lacked them until then. The
clearing step is upstream's; this release does not change it.

No live game or save was changed for this release.
