## Changes

- **Sorcerers can equip daggers, quarterstaves, darts and slings without
  proficiency pips again**, like mages. This affects every sorcerer kit, the
  Dragon Disciple included, whenever the EEex weapon usability is installed.
  Since upstream commit
  [7464043](https://github.com/TheArtisanBG/The-Artisan-s-Kitpack/commit/7464043fbf93fbd448401436181713de3737b72e)
  (February 21), the class weapon grants in `C0PR#CL.SPL` are generated from
  the columns of `WEAPPROF.2DA`. That table has no sorcerer column, because the
  engine gives sorcerers the mage column, so sorcerers received no class
  weapons at all. Sorcerers now use the mage column in both the first-run
  generator (`ArtisansKitpack/eeex/profs_eeex.tpa`) and the "Update EEex-based
  Weapon Usability Changes" tweak (`ArtisansKitpack/lib/profs_eeex_patch.tpa`).
  Mages and all other classes keep their grants unchanged.
- **Existing installations** can append the new
  [AKCB_SORCERER_WEAPONS](../../live-patch/AKCB_SORCERER_WEAPONS/README.md)
  tail instead of reinstalling. It adds the same sorcerer grants to the
  installed `C0PR#CL.SPL` and keeps everything else in that file. Saves need no
  edits: characters receive the grants the next time `C0PR#CL` is applied to
  them, at their next level-up at the latest.

The previously released balance changes are unchanged.
