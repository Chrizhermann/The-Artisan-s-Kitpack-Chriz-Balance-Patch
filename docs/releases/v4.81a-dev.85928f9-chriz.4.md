# v4.81a-dev.85928f9-chriz.4

This release lets monks equip quarterstaves and holds shamans without a kit,
and characters of kits that a component adds late, to their weapons from the
start, with the EEex-based weapon usability. It changes new installations
only. CEBG manages its artifact pin separately; this owner release does not
update the collection recipe or any installed game.

## Changes

- **Monks: quarterstaves.** Monk Revisions (component `10001`) lets monks put
  proficiency points into quarterstaves in `WEAPPROF.2DA`. With the EEex-based
  weapon usability, `C0PR#CL.SPL` grants each class the weapons that its
  `WEAPPROF.2DA` column allows, but Kitpack wrote those class grants only when
  its generator first ran. When Monk Revisions came later, as in CEBG's order,
  a monk without quarterstaff pips could not equip a quarterstaff unless the
  "Update EEex-based Weapon Usability Changes" tweak (`1010000`) was installed
  afterwards. Monk Revisions now writes the class grants again after its
  change, so monks are granted quarterstaves in either order.
- **Shamans: restricted from the start.** Joining the party and every level-up
  apply `C0PR#CL` through an "apply `C0PR#CL`" entry in the character's ability
  table. Kitpack added it to the tables of the other classes and of all kits,
  but not to the shaman's own table (`CLABSH01.2DA`). A shaman without a kit
  could therefore equip every weapon after joining the party, until Kitpack's
  helper creature applied `C0PR#CL` (after loading a save or on entering an
  area), and its level-ups did not apply `C0PR#CL`. The generator now adds the
  entry to `CLABSH01.2DA` at every level as well and keeps the table's other
  entries.
- **Kits added late: restricted from the start.** Only the components that
  include the generator gave ability tables the "apply `C0PR#CL`" entry. A kit
  or ability table that a Kitpack component added or replaced after the last
  of them had none, so `C0PR#CL` did not restrict its characters after joining
  the party until the helper creature applied it, and their level-ups did not
  apply it. Examples: the Favored Soul's own kit, which its component (`30001`) adds
  after including the generator; the Sacred Fist, the Enlightened Fist
  (`10003`) and the Ninja (`10004`) when they come after the Favored Soul.
  The main installer also widens ability tables with fewer than 50 levels
  before each of its components, which left levels 41 to 50 of such tables
  without the entry, and the "Dual-Classes Keep Kit Names in Character
  Record" tweak (`1020000`) took it out of every fighter, cleric, thief, druid
  and ranger kit table. Now every component of the main installer ends by
  giving every class and kit ability table the entry at every level, and so
  do the NPC components that add kits (`2001` Rashemi Berserker for Minsc,
  `5102` Red Wizard for Edwin) and the tweak `1020000`. This writes only the
  tables it changes and adds about a quarter of a second per component.
- **One place for both.** The class grants and the ability table entries now
  come from `ArtisansKitpack/eeex/class_weapon_grants.tpa`, which the generator
  (`eeex/profs_eeex.tpa`), the tweak (`lib/profs_eeex_patch.tpa`), Monk
  Revisions and the installers call. New class grants take the place of the
  earlier ones, so effects that other mods added to `C0PR#CL` keep their
  place, and "apply `C0PR#KG`" stays its last effect. The tweak now also adds
  the entry to ability tables that mods installed after Kitpack added or
  replaced. Running any of them again without changes changes nothing.
- **Existing installations** are not changed by this release, and there is no
  tail for it.

The previously released balance, sorcerer and kit weapon changes are unchanged.

## Validation and installation boundary

October 27 results: all 133 offline tests passed, including 15 new WeiDU tests
in synthetic games and 7 checks of the installers. The WeiDU tests run the real
generator and tweak, the real quarterstaff change of Monk Revisions, the real
`extend_tables.tpa` and the lines that Monk Revisions and the installers now
run, in several install orders. The model of the weapon grant opcodes shows
the missing quarterstaff before the fix and the same monk weapons in both
orders after it. They also cover a shaman table in the vanilla layout, a
missing one and one that a later mod replaces, kits added after the
generator, a 40-level table that a component puts in place and a later one
widens to 50 levels, an entry taken out of a kit table, installs without the
EEex-based weapon usability, effects of other mods in `C0PR#CL`, and repeated
runs, which write no file. The installer checks find the pass at the end of
every main component and of the NPC and tweak components that add kits or
rewrite ability tables. All three installer versions match the release
identity.

In-game lab check, also on October 27: three disposable BG2:EE 2.7.3.0 games
with EEex 1.3.1, each a new copy of the same test game, with Kitpack installed
by WeiDU 249.

- **Before:** `v4.81a-dev.85928f9-chriz.3`, after Spell Revisions, with the 47
  Kitpack components and 7 tweaks that CEBG installs, in CEBG's order.
- **CEBG's order:** this release, installed the same way, with the two NPC
  components that add kits (`2001`, `5102`) between the main components and
  the tweaks, where CEBG installs them.
- **Another order:** this release without Spell Revisions. First the other
  components, then the tweaks, then the Favored Soul (`30001`), then Monk
  Revisions (`10001`) with the Sacred Fist and Enlightened Fist (`10003`) and
  Ninja (`10004`) components, then the two NPC components, and last the tweak
  `1020000`. Here the Ninja is a kit added after the last component that
  includes the generator.

In each game, new level-1 characters, a monk and a shaman without a kit and a
Favored Soul, each with a single proficiency pip in sling, moved a dagger, a
quarterstaff, a mace, a battle axe and a long sword from the backpack into a
weapon slot in the inventory screen. In the two games with this release, a
Ninja did the same. They did this after joining the party, after Kitpack's
helper creature had appeared again, after a level-up to level 2 and after
saving and loading.

- Before: the game refused the quarterstaff to the monk each time ("Cannot use
  item."). The shaman equipped all five weapons after joining. Once the helper
  creature had appeared, it equipped only the dagger, the quarterstaff and the
  battle axe.
- With this release, in both orders, the monk equipped the quarterstaff each
  time. From joining on, the shaman equipped only the dagger, the quarterstaff
  and the battle axe, and the game refused it the mace and the long sword. The
  Ninja equipped the dagger, the quarterstaff and the long sword each time, and
  the game refused it the mace and the battle axe.
- In all three games, the Favored Soul equipped the dagger, the quarterstaff and
  the mace, and the game refused it the battle axe and the long sword. The
  monk's results for the other weapons were also the same in all three games.

The weapon grant effects show the same. Right after joining the party, before
the helper creature appeared again, the shaman of the game before this release
still had the grants for every weapon type that it was spawned with. With this
release, in both orders, `C0PR#CL` had already replaced the spawn grants of all
four characters with those of their class and kit. The grant effects were also
marked before the level-up. Before this release, the shaman still had its
marked grant effects afterwards: its level-up did not apply `C0PR#CL`. With
this release, in both orders, the level-up replaced every marked grant effect
of all four characters with a new one. For the Ninja, the effects are the
evidence: the mace and the battle axe themselves are unusable by monks, so its
weapon results alone would look the same without `C0PR#CL`.

The installed files agree. Before this release, `C0PR#CL` granted monks no
quarterstaff, although `WEAPPROF.2DA` gives them five quarterstaff pips after
Monk Revisions, and `CLABSH01.2DA` had no "apply `C0PR#CL`" entry. With this
release, in both orders, `C0PR#CL` grants monks the quarterstaff and keeps
"apply `C0PR#KG`" as its last effect, and all 73 class and kit ability tables,
those of the Sacred Fist, the Enlightened Fist, the Ninja and the two NPC kits
included, apply `C0PR#CL` at levels 1 to 50. In the 33 kit tables that the
tweak `1020000` changes, the entry stayed, and the tweak only added its own
entries. In another game, an earlier build of this release that did not yet
end each component with the table pass left the Sacred Fist, Enlightened Fist
and Ninja tables without the entry at every level in the other order. The two
orders gave `C0PR#CL` the same class grants. Only the `SPLPROT.2DA` row numbers
of its proficiency checks differ, because Spell Revisions, installed first in
CEBG's order, adds rows to that table before Kitpack does.

Not covered in the game: EET, kits other than the Favored Soul and the Ninja,
the Ninja before this release, loading a save in a new game process, and
characters created before installing this release.

No live game or save was changed for this release.
