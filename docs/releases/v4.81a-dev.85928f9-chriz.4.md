# v4.81a-dev.85928f9-chriz.4

This release lets monks equip quarterstaves and holds shamans without a kit to
the shaman's weapons from the start, with the EEex-based weapon usability. It
changes new installations only. CEBG manages its artifact pin separately; this
owner release does not update the collection recipe or any installed game.

## Changes

- **Monks: quarterstaves.** Monk Revisions (component `10001`) lets monks put
  proficiency points into quarterstaves in `WEAPPROF.2DA`. With the EEex-based
  weapon usability, `C0PR#CL.SPL` grants each class the weapons that its
  `WEAPPROF.2DA` column allows, but Kitpack wrote those class grants only when
  its generator first ran. When Monk Revisions came later, as in CEBG's order,
  a monk without quarterstaff pips could not equip a quarterstaff unless the
  "Update EEex-based Weapon Usability Changes" tweak (`1010000`) was installed
  afterwards. Monk Revisions now writes the class grants again after its
  change, so monks are granted quarterstaves in either order.
- **Shamans: restricted from the start.** Joining the party and every level-up
  apply `C0PR#CL` through an "apply `C0PR#CL`" entry in the character's ability
  table. Kitpack added it to the tables of the other classes and of all kits,
  but not to the shaman's own table (`CLABSH01.2DA`). A shaman without a kit
  could therefore equip every weapon after joining the party and after a
  level-up, until Kitpack's helper creature applied `C0PR#CL` (after loading a
  save or on entering an area). The generator now adds the entry to
  `CLABSH01.2DA` at every level as well and keeps the table's other entries.
  The Favored Soul component (`30001`) also adds it to its own kit's table right
  after creating the kit; until now the Favored Soul only got it from a later
  component that includes the generator, such as the tweak `8204` in CEBG's
  order.
- **One place for both.** The class grants and the ability table entries now
  come from `ArtisansKitpack/eeex/class_weapon_grants.tpa`, which the generator
  (`eeex/profs_eeex.tpa`), the tweak (`lib/profs_eeex_patch.tpa`), Monk
  Revisions and the Favored Soul component call. New class grants take the
  place of the earlier ones, so effects that other mods added to `C0PR#CL` keep
  their place, and "apply `C0PR#KG`" stays its last effect. The tweak now also
  adds the entry to ability tables that mods installed after Kitpack added or
  replaced. Running any of them again without changes changes nothing.
- **Existing installations** are not changed by this release, and there is no
  tail for it.

The previously released balance, sorcerer and kit weapon changes are unchanged.

## Validation and installation boundary

October 27 results: all 124 offline tests passed, including 13 new WeiDU tests
in synthetic games. They run the real generator and tweak, the real
quarterstaff change of Monk Revisions and the lines that Monk Revisions and the
Favored Soul component now run, with the generator before and after Monk
Revisions. The model of the weapon grant opcodes shows the missing quarterstaff
before the fix and the same monk weapons in both orders after it. They also
cover a shaman table in the vanilla layout, a missing one and one that a later
mod replaces, kits added after the generator, effects of other mods in
`C0PR#CL`, repeated runs, and where the new calls sit. All three installer
versions match the release identity.

The in-game lab check is recorded after it has run.

No live game or save was changed for this release.
