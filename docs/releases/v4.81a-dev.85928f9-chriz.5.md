# v4.81a-dev.85928f9-chriz.5

This release lets the Invisible Blade and Bardic Wonders' Dancer use the
katanas they may train and the rogue and bard items the item files closed to
them by mistake. Like the Elementalist, they cannot wear armor because they
borrow the Shapeshifter's item usability bit, and the item files also set that
bit wherever druids may not use an item. The Elementalist, a druid kit, stays
blocked from all of these. The release changes new installations only. CEBG
manages its artifact pin separately; this owner release does not update the
collection recipe or any installed game.

## Changes

- **Kits without armor through the Shapeshifter's bit.** The Invisible Blade
  (component `7005`), the Elementalist (`5001`) and the Dancer of Bardic
  Wonders get "may not wear armor" from `KITLIST.2DA`: their `UNUSABLE` value
  is the Shapeshifter's, `0x10000000`, and the game's body armor carries that
  bit. The item files also set the bits of all druid kits wherever druids may
  not use an item: every katana, the Rogue's Potion of Frost Giant Strength
  and some mage scrolls carry the bit next to the Totemic Druid's and the
  Avenger's. So the Invisible Blade could not equip a katana it may put
  proficiency points into, nor drink the potion made for thieves and bards,
  and the Dancer could neither equip katanas nor read those scrolls. Copies
  of such items keep the bit: Tweaks Anthology's two-handed katanas
  (`c!kt<N>`/`cdkt<N>`) and SCS's drow katana `DW#DWKAT`.
- **The fix.** `ArtisansKitpack/lib/shapeshifter_kit_bit.tpa` takes the bit
  off items other than body armor where it only stands for the druid kits,
  and keeps every kit it still has to block blocked through an exact kit
  check (opcode 319, "not usable by" this `KIT.IDS` value, marked with the
  resource `C0SHAPE_`):
  - A kit that borrows the bit is freed from it on an item when the item
    blocks none of the kits the game made for its class (for thieves the
    Assassin, Bounty Hunter, Swashbuckler and Shadowdancer, for bards the
    Blade, Jester and Skald). Items that only a few kits may use block those
    kits too, so there the bit keeps blocking, and so does an item the
    borrowing kit's class may not use anyway.
  - Druid kits are never freed: the Shapeshifter and the Elementalist keep
    every item they could not use, the Shapeshifter through its own kit
    check where the bit goes. Neither are kits of classes without kits of
    their own in that list (shamans, multi-classes) nor kits that carry
    another of their class's kit bits as well.
  - Every kit that is not freed gets the pass's own check where the bit goes,
    also when another kit bit or another mod's check blocks it now: those may
    go later, the bit would not have.
  - Body armor keeps the bit everywhere: the borrowed rule itself. So does an
    item whose equipped effects end inside an ability's effects: no check
    could be added to the one list without the other.
  The Invisible Blade and Elementalist components free the items present
  when they are installed. The new tweak `7205`, "Invisible Blade - Apply the
  Katana Fix to Items and Kits Added Later", does the same for items and kits
  that mods installed afterwards added, and so does the "Update EEex-based
  Weapon Usability Changes" tweak (`1010000`) at its end. Both add the checks
  for a kit added since to the items an earlier run changed. Running the pass
  again without changes changes nothing.
- **Existing installations** are not changed by this release, and there is no
  tail for it.

The previously released balance, sorcerer, kit weapon, monk and shaman
changes are unchanged.

## Validation and installation boundary

October 29 results: all 144 offline tests passed, including 8 new WeiDU tests
in synthetic games and 3 checks of the installers. The WeiDU tests run the pass
through the installers' own lines (the components `5001`, `7005` and the
tweak `7205`) and cover katanas with and without Kitpack's EEex proficiency
gate, the Rogue's potion, a mage scroll as BG2:EE ships it and as Spell
Revisions and Kitpack leave it, body armor, an item only a few kits may use,
an item the borrowing kit's class may not use, an item that already has
another mod's check for the Shapeshifter, equipped effects before and after an
ability's effects and between the effects of two abilities, abilities behind
the effect table, an empty equipped list whose index points into an ability's
effects, empty lists whose indices point past the end of the file,
overlapping effect lists and an ability whose effects run past the end of the
file (both left alone), the Wild Mage's bit 31, a `KITLIST.2DA` without its
RESERVE row, short and non-V1 files, the hidden GRIZZLY_BEAR kit, a shaman kit
and a thief kit with a second kit bit, Bardic Wonders' Dancer added after the
Invisible Blade, the Elementalist added before and after it, a copy of a fixed
katana and a katana a later mod adds, repeated runs (no file changes) and
uninstalling (every file as before).

October 29 in-game check: three disposable BG2:EE 2.7.3.0 labs with EEex
1.3.1 passed, one with Spell Revisions, one without and one in another layout
(only the Dancer component of Bardic Wonders, no other tweaks, the "Update
EEex-based Weapon Usability Changes" tweak instead of `7205`). They held this
fork at `c146502`, Bardic Wonders `v2.9c-balance.8`, Tweaks Anthology v18's
two-handed katanas and a stand-in that adds SCS's drow katana file. An
Invisible Blade, a Dancer, a Bounty Hunter, a Shapeshifter, an Elementalist
and an unkitted druid tried each item when they joined, with a forced katana
proficiency, after a level-up and after saving and loading:
- The Invisible Blade and the Dancer equipped the katanas (`SW1H43`, a
  two-handed `C!KT1`, SCS's drow katana once `7205` or the EEex tweak ran) and
  drank the Rogue's potion. After `7205` the game showed the Dancer `SCRL1W`
  and `SCRL2B` as usable scrolls and took them into a quick slot. Neither
  kit could wear leather armor, and the Invisible Blade still could not use
  mage scrolls, as thieves may not.
- The Shapeshifter and the Elementalist were refused every katana in every
  phase, also with the forced proficiency that lets the EEex proficiency gate
  pass (30 of 30 and 15 of 15 attempts), while a copy without the kit checks
  was equipped then and refused without the proficiency. The unkitted druid
  was refused the katanas without the proficiency and equipped them with it.
  So a kit check blocks even where the gate allows. The Bounty Hunter could
  use every item but the mage scrolls.
- Removing `7205` in the Spell Revisions lab restored all 12,940 override
  files and `dialog.tlk` byte for byte.

The labs ran the first commit. What changed since cannot change their
results: the extra checks go only to kits that other kit bits of the same
items already block (here the hidden GRIZZLY_BEAR kit), and no item of the
labs, of a full CEBG EEex 1.3.1 lab or of a played CEBG game has another
mod's Shapeshifter check or a layout the new guards concern.

Not covered: EET, Kitpack's NPC installer, installs without EEex in a game,
the pass before any EEex proficiency sweep in a game (CEBG's Eldritch Knight
component already sweeps before the kits), casting from the freed scrolls,
other katanas and SCS's potion copy in a game, and Spell Revisions in the
third layout.
